1How often should gutters be cleaned?
About 2 times a year. Your location and how close the trees are will set your own schedule, and gutter guards dramatically reduce how often.
2What is included in a gutter installation?
Removing ineffective gutter guards, installing new gutters where needed, cleaning and repair, re-aligning and sealing, internal hangers, downspouts, all materials and labor, and a final cleanup of the job site.
3When is it time to replace gutters instead of repairing them?
If you are consistently needing repairs, it may be time for new gutters. Gutters with 6 or more areas of cracks, holes or rust spots may be better off replaced, and so may gutters with broken fasteners, nails or screws on the ground, or overall separation.
4What happens to my old gutters?
We take them down and dispose of them as part of the installation, at no extra charge.
5What is covered by the warranty?
Our labor is covered for 10 years. Materials carry a 10-year to lifetime warranty.
6What is the difference between hiring a professional gutter cleaner and doing it myself?
While it may seem like a more affordable option to clean your gutters on your own, the potential risks simply do not outweigh the savings. You risk serious injuries and might not own the proper equipment or experience.
7Do gutter guards keep out animals?
Yes, the gutter guard system is designed to keep out animals, insects, pine needles, leaves and other types of debris.
8How does gutter drainage work?
The underground drainage pipes re-direct the water away from the home, usually to the street or the sidewalk, in order to avoid water damage to the home or the foundation.
9How do I know if my underground drain is clogged?
You might start noticing damp patches in your basement. If wastewater cannot make its way to the sewer due to a blockage, it is likely to end up there, causing damage such as mouldy surfaces or even structural damage to the walls or floor.
